Aspio Product Demo

Recording script: screen capture with voice-over

Calm, enterprise-friendly toneAbout 10–12 minStart: app.aspio.com/dashboard
ACTION: do on screenSAY: read aloudCAPTION: overlay text

Pre-recording checklist

  • Browser at 100% zoom, 1920×1080, notifications off.
  • Demo org pre-loaded with realistic data (branches, payslips, leave requests).
  • Unused email ready; verification and invite emails open in a second tab.
  • Confirm Inventory, HR, Company Settings, Branding, Shift Reminders and Export are reachable (they are not in the sidebar list).
  • Do one silent dry run. Pause about one second after each click.
Production notes
